## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VolTap ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Information the App Collects and Stores Locally

VolTap stores the following data **locally on your device only**. This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party by us:

- **Volume profiles and settings:** Your custom volume profiles, scheduled profile rules, floating button preferences (size, opacity, color, position), and general app settings.
- **Per-app volume preferences:** The volume levels you configure for individual apps.
- **Bluetooth device information:** The names and MAC addresses of Bluetooth devices you connect to, along with your saved volume preferences for each device. This data is used solely to restore your preferred volume when you reconnect to a known device.

**We do not operate any servers that receive data from the App. All user-configured data remains on your device.**

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google LLC and may collect certain information automatically, including:

- **Device identifiers:** Advertising ID, Android ID
- **Approximate location:** Derived from IP address
- **Ad interaction data:** Information about ads viewed and interacted with
- **Device information:** Device model, operating system version, and similar technical data

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. We do not have access to or control over the data Google collects through AdMob.

For more information, please review:
- Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use their services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Accessibility Service Usage

VolTap requests access to Android's Accessibility Service **solely to detect which application is currently in the foreground**. This is required for the per-app volume control feature, which automatically adjusts volume levels based on the active app.

**The Accessibility Service is used only to read the foreground app's package name.** VolTap does NOT:
- Read, collect, or store the content of any windows or screens
- Monitor, log, or record your interactions with other apps
- Capture text, images, or any on-screen information
- Transmit any accessibility data to any server or third party

You can disable the Accessibility Service at any time through your device's Settings > Accessibility menu. Doing so will disable the per-app volume feature, but all other App features will continue to work.

## Bluetooth Permission Usage

VolTap uses the Bluetooth permission to detect when Bluetooth audio devices (headphones, speakers, car stereos) connect to or disconnect from your device. The App stores the device name and MAC address locally to associate your preferred volume settings with each device.

This information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted externally.

## Data Retention

All data generated by VolTap is stored locally in the App's private storage on your device. Data is retained for as long as the App is installed. **Uninstalling VolTap permanently removes all stored data**, including your volume profiles, per-app settings, Bluetooth device preferences, and all other configurations.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the following options to manage your data:

- **Clear all App data:** Go to Android Settings > Apps > VolTap > Storage > Clear Data. This removes all locally stored settings and preferences.
- **Reset Advertising ID:** You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads through your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.
- **Disable Accessibility Service:** Go to Settings > Accessibility and disable VolTap's service to stop foreground app detection.
- **Revoke permissions:** You can revoke any permission granted to VolTap through Android Settings > Apps > VolTap > Permissions.

## Children's Privacy

VolTap is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, which may show personalized ads based on device information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child is using this App, you can clear all App data or uninstall the App to remove any locally stored information. You may also configure your child's device to limit ad personalization through the device's privacy settings.

## Data Security

All data stored by VolTap resides in the App's private internal storage directory, which is protected by the Android operating system and is not accessible to other apps. No user data is transmitted over the internet by the App itself. Data transmitted by the AdMob SDK to Google's servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
