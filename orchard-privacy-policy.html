Orchard - Minesweeper Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 19, 2026
Thank you for playing Orchard - Minesweeper Puzzle ("Orchard", "we", "our", or "the app"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect (if any), how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. By using Orchard, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Orchard is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app.

All game data — including your progress, streaks, statistics, preferences, and puzzle history — is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, collect, or store any of this data on external servers.

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or other personally identifiable information
  • Location data
  • Contact lists or phone data
  • Usage analytics beyond what is provided by third-party ad services

2. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to use, share, or sell. Your game data remains on your device and is under your control.

If you choose to share your puzzle results (for example, sharing a Wordle-style grid with friends), that sharing is initiated entirely by you through your device's native sharing capabilities. We do not intercept, store, or transmit any shared content.

3. Third-Party Services

Orchard uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: We display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free availability of the app. By default, ads shown are non-personalized. AdMob may collect certain device-level information (such as advertising ID and device type) in accordance with Google's privacy policy. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): We use Google UMP to manage consent for users in regions where regulations such as the GDPR (European Union) or CCPA (California, USA) apply. This platform allows you to control whether personalized advertisements are shown to you.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver optional push notifications, such as daily puzzle reminders. FCM generates a device-level token to deliver notifications; this token is not linked to any personal identity. You can disable notifications at any time from the app's settings or your device's notification settings.

We do not use any other third-party analytics, tracking, or data collection services.

4. Data Storage

All game-related data is stored locally on your device using standard Android storage mechanisms. This data includes:

  • Game progress and puzzle completion history
  • Streak records and statistics
  • User preferences (such as sound settings and colorblind mode)
  • Hint usage

This data is not backed up to any external server by us. If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted.

5. Children's Privacy

Orchard is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect any personal information from any user, no special data collection concerns apply to younger users.

Advertisements displayed through Google AdMob comply with Google's policies regarding ad content. Non-personalized ads are shown by default.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of Orchard after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
